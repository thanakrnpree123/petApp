PawHealth helps you keep track of your pets' health. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the PawHealth apps for iOS and Android and to the web app.

PawHealth is operated by [LEGAL NAME OR COMPANY], [ADDRESS] ("we", "us"). We are the data controller under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA). Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

DataWhat it includesWhy
Account Email address and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it), your app language, and when you signed up. To create your account, sign you in, and show the app in your language.
Pet profiles What you enter about each pet: name, species, breed, birth date, sex, neutered status, microchip number, allergies, weight, and a photo if you add one. To provide the app: profiles, reminders and reports.
Pet health records Weight entries, notes, vaccinations with their dates, and the answers and results of symptom checks you run. To show your pet's history and trends, remind you of due vaccines, and create PDF reports you choose to share.
Subscription Whether you have PawHealth Plus, and how many free symptom checks you have used this month. Payment details go only to Apple or Google; we never receive your card details. To provide Plus features and apply the free-plan limit.
On your device only Your language choice, whether you have seen certain prompts, and scheduled vaccine reminders. These stay on your device. To remember your settings and show reminders.

Information about your pets is not personal data in itself, but we treat it as yours and protect it the same way.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Camera, photos and notifications

PawHealth asks for camera or photo library access only when you choose to add a pet photo, and uses it only for that photo. Notification permission is used only for the vaccine reminders you set up. You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

Legal bases

  • Contract: to provide the app and the features you ask for, including subscriptions.
  • Consent: for camera, photo and notification access, which you can withdraw in your device settings.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the service secure and working, for example to prevent abuse.
  • Legal obligation: to keep purchase records where the law requires it.

Who we share data with

We use these service providers to run PawHealth. They process data on our behalf and only for these purposes:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): sign-in, the database and photo storage. Your account and pet data are stored on Google's servers[, located in REGION].
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.): manages subscriptions bought in the iOS and Android apps. It receives your account ID (a random identifier, not your email) and your purchase history.
  • Apple and Google process payments for subscriptions under their own privacy policies.
  • Google Fonts: the app downloads its fonts from Google, which sees your device's IP address when it does.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the web app, and sees your IP address when you load it.

Some providers are outside Thailand, including in the United States. Where the PDPA requires it, we make sure these transfers are protected, for example by the providers' standard contractual safeguards. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, pets, health records, symptom checks and photos are deleted straight away. Copies in Google's backup systems are erased on Google's schedule, typically within about six months. Purchase records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat are kept under their policies and billing or tax laws; see how to delete your account.

Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask to access or get a copy of your data, have it corrected, move it to another service, object to or restrict how we use it, have it deleted, and withdraw consent. You can edit or delete most data yourself in the app, and delete your whole account in Settings → Delete account. For anything else, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. You may also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC). If you are in the EU or UK, you have similar rights under the GDPR.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google Firebase, and database rules allow only you to read or change your own account and pet data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.
