Delete your PawHealth account

You can delete your PawHealth account and its data at any time. This can't be undone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open PawHealth (iOS, Android or the web app) and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Can't sign in?

Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you used for PawHealth, with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days [confirm the time frame]. If you forgot your password, you can also reset it from the sign-in screen and then delete the account in the app.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, sign-in and settings
  • All your pet profiles and photos
  • Weight and health records, vaccinations and symptom-check history
  • Vaccine reminders scheduled on the device you delete from

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups: copies in Google Firebase's backup systems are erased on Google's schedule, typically within about six months.
  • Subscription records: Apple or Google, and our subscription provider RevenueCat, keep records of PawHealth Plus purchases for billing, tax and fraud prevention, under their own policies. These records carry a random account ID, not your email. To also delete the RevenueCat record, email [CONTACT EMAIL].
  • Reminders on other devices: they stay until you sign out there or uninstall the app.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel PawHealth Plus. Cancel it first: on iOS in your App Store account settings, and on Android in Google Play › Subscriptions.
