Common questions

How do I cancel PawHealth Plus?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought them from. On an iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → PawHealth. On Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → PawHealth. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel the subscription.

I bought Plus but the app doesn't show it

Open the Plus screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple or Google account you bought it with. Plus bought in the iOS or Android app may not unlock the web app yet.

Vaccine reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for PawHealth in your phone's settings, and that the vaccine has a next due date. Reminders arrive at 9:00 the day before the due date. They are set on each phone separately, so sign in on a new phone to set them up there.

Can I trust the symptom checker?

It gives general guidance on how urgent a problem might be. It is not a diagnosis. If you're unsure, contact your vet.

How do I change the app's language?

Settings → Language: English, ไทย or 中文.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete account. See how to delete your account for other ways and what is removed.
